Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This is a working draft of HiLouder's privacy policy, published so guests and restaurant partners can see our intent before launch. It will be reviewed by counsel and finalized before any guest data is collected in production.

What we collect

To operate the guest retention system, HiLouder collects the information a guest voluntarily provides when joining a restaurant's text list (such as a phone number and, optionally, a name), along with basic usage data needed to run campaigns and track redemptions.

How we use it

  • To add a guest to the restaurant's own guest list
  • To send the SMS campaigns that restaurant chooses to send
  • To track which promotions lead to a redemption

Who owns the data

Guest lists belong to the restaurant that collected them. HiLouder does not sell guest data or share it across restaurants.

Questions

For any privacy question, reach us through the contact form.
