SMS Program Terms

Last updated: August 2026

This is a working draft of HiLouder's SMS program terms, published so guests and restaurant partners can see our intent before launch. It will be reviewed by counsel and finalized — including carrier-required language — before SMS activation.

Consent

Guests join a restaurant's text list by scanning a QR code and submitting their phone number, an affirmative opt-in action. No guest is added to a list without taking that action themselves.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies by restaurant and campaign. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out and help

Guests can reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from a restaurant's list, and HELP for support. Both keywords are handled automatically as part of the platform.

Who sends the messages

Each restaurant using HiLouder sends its own campaigns to its own guest list — guest data and messaging are never shared across restaurants.

Questions

For any question about SMS terms, reach us through the contact form.
