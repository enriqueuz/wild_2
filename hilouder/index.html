FAQ

Questions restaurant owners usually ask

Do I need to replace my POS?

No. HiLouder is designed to work alongside your existing POS and restaurant tools.

What exactly does the guest see?

They scan your QR code, access your digital menu and can choose to join your restaurant's text list.

Do guests have to download an app?

No. The experience is designed around the phone and SMS.

Can I see which promotions actually worked?

Yes. Promotion codes let you track campaign-driven sales.

How do customers unsubscribe?

STOP/HELP handling and the required SMS compliance controls are built into the system.

How long does setup take?

Your platform and menu can be prepared within a few business days (typically 2–3); SMS activation takes a bit longer — up to 7 additional business days — because of carrier verification.

Can I cancel?

We'll walk through the exact commercial terms on your free call so there are no surprises.

Do you write the campaigns for us?

Your plan gets you set up with a welcome message and your first campaigns. Ongoing campaign writing can be discussed on your free call.
